# BIOBUZZ™ — 2026-2027 FTC Game Rules Summary

Summary of the *FIRST® Tech Challenge Competition Manual*, BIOBUZZ™ presented by RTX, **Version TU01**.
Source: https://ftc-resources.firstinspires.org/ftc/game/manual

This is a condensed reference for the team. When a call matters, check the full manual — the Head REFEREE's ruling is final and no video is ever reviewed.

---

## 1. Game Overview

Two ALLIANCES of two teams each (red and blue) collect **POLLEN** (yellow balls) and **NECTAR** (red/blue balls), **LAUNCH** them into their own **HIVE** to tip it, and place them into **FLOWERS**.

- **AUTO (30 s):** robots run autonomously. Collect POLLEN, LAUNCH into the upward-facing CELL of your HIVE. Enough scoring elements in the CELL causes a **HIVE TIP** — points are earned and one more NECTAR is unlocked for the human player.
- **TELEOP (2:00):** drivers take over. Keep tipping the HIVE and unlocking NECTAR.
- **Last 60 seconds:** all remaining NECTAR may be entered, and NECTAR may be placed into FLOWERS. ALLIANCES fight for **FLOWER ownership** (top-most NECTAR of your color) and the **Bottom NECTAR bonus**, then PARK in the LOADING ZONE.

Most match points wins. Extra RANKING POINTS (RP) come from LEAVE/PARK and TIP-count thresholds.

## 2. Match Structure

| Phase | Duration | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-match setup | — | Field staff stage elements; drive teams stage robots and consoles |
| **AUTO** | 0:30 | No driver input. Red owns field columns A–C, blue owns D–F |
| Transition | 0:08 | Robots must be **motionless** (no powered movement) |
| **TELEOP** | 2:00 | Drivers control robots |
| FLOWER ownership unlock | at 1:00 remaining | NECTAR may now enter FLOWERS; all remaining NECTAR may be entered |
| Match end | 0:00 | Robots must be motionless until referee signals |

The **visual field timer** is authoritative; audio cues are a courtesy.

## 3. Field & Elements

**Field:** ~144 in × 144 in (12 ft square), 36 soft foam tiles (24 in each). Red ALLIANCE AREA is on the audience's left.

### Zones
- **ALLIANCE AREA** — ~97 in × 54 in outside the field where the DRIVE TEAM stands.
- **LOADING ZONE** — ~23 in × 11 in, alliance-specific, at the field wall adjacent to the alliance area. Where NECTAR is introduced and where robots PARK.
- **GARDEN** — ~23 in × 2 in tape strip in opposite corners (red and blue). *Not* a protected zone.

### HIVE Structure (center of field)
- One frame holds a **red HIVE** and a **blue HIVE**. Pivot axis is 43.95 in above tiles.
- Each HIVE has **2 CELLS** (one on each end) and is **bi-stable** — one CELL faces up, the other down. LAUNCHING enough elements into the upward CELL tips it over, dumping its contents onto the floor and raising the other CELL.
- CELL opening: ~20 in wide × 14 in tall × 12 in deep.
- A **HIVE TIP** counts when the HIVE moves from one stable state to the other and the damper contacts the frame.
- Each CELL's underside has an **AprilTag cluster** (4 tags, 36h11 family, 3.25 in). Blue audience-side CELL = IDs 38–41; red audience-side ends at ID 7; red far-side starts at ID 0. (Some IDs did not survive PDF extraction — confirm in the manual / field resources.)

### FLOWERS (4, mounted on the perimeter wall)
- Top opening ~4 in diameter, ~21.5 in above tiles, with a 1.25 in backstop.
- **Scoring volume:** between the top ring and middle ring.
- **Retrieval opening** at the bottom (~3.55 in tall) — only **POLLEN** can be removed from the bottom; NECTAR cannot.

### Scoring Elements
| Element | Size | Count | Color |
|---|---|---|---|
| POLLEN | ~2.8 in ball | 40 | yellow |
| NECTAR | ~3.6 in ball | 8 red + 8 blue | red / blue |

Balls are not perfectly spherical and vary in size — design intakes with tolerance.

### Starting Layout
- **POLLEN (40):** 4 in each FLOWER (16), 4 in red GARDEN, 4 in blue GARDEN, **4 pre-loaded in/on each robot** (16).
- **NECTAR (16):** 3 in each alliance's upward-facing CELL (6), 5 in each ALLIANCE AREA with the human player (10).
- HIVEs start tilted; the CELL that "points at" a FLOWER is the one tilted down.

## 4. Scoring

### Point Values (Table 10-2)

| Achievement | AUTO | TELEOP |
|---|---|---|
| **LEAVE** (robot no longer touching perimeter wall, assessed end of AUTO) | 3 | — |
| **PARK** (robot at least partially in LOADING ZONE) | 5 | 5 |
| **HIVE TIP** | **20** | **20** |
| POLLEN/NECTAR remaining in upward CELL at end of match (each) | — | 2 |
| **Bottom NECTAR Bonus** (bottom-most scored NECTAR in a FLOWER is yours) | — | 5 |
| POLLEN/NECTAR in a FLOWER you **own** (each, regardless of who placed it) | — | 2 |
| POLLEN/NECTAR in your GARDEN (each, regardless of who placed it) | — | 1 |

### Ranking Points (qualification matches)

| RP | Condition | RP |
|---|---|---|
| **WIN** | More match points than opponent | 3 |
| **TIE** | Equal match points | 1 |
| **SWARM RP** | Combined LEAVE + PARK points ≥ threshold | 1 |
| **POLLINATOR 1 RP** | Number of TIPS ≥ threshold | 1 |
| **POLLINATOR 2 RP** | Number of TIPS ≥ threshold | 1 |

**Thresholds (all regular events):** SWARM = **16 points**, POLLINATOR 1 = **4 TIPS**, POLLINATOR 2 = **7 TIPS**. Regional/FIRST Championship thresholds TBA via Team Updates.

Max possible RP per match = 6 (WIN + 3 bonus RPs).

### Scoring Details
- **HIVE TIPS** are counted throughout the match. Tips completed before TELEOP starts count as AUTO. LAUNCHING into the upward CELL is the *only* legal way to cause a tip.
- **FLOWER ownership:** the alliance whose NECTAR is **top-most** in a FLOWER's scoring volume owns it and scores 2 pts for *every* element in that FLOWER. Bottom-most NECTAR earns a 5 pt bonus. NECTAR cannot enter FLOWERS until 1:00 remains (G410).
- **GARDEN:** any POLLEN or NECTAR (either color) at least partially in a GARDEN scores 1 pt for the GARDEN's color. Either alliance can remove elements from either GARDEN.
- **NECTAR unlock:** humans may enter one NECTAR per HIVE TIP of their color, and all remaining NECTAR once 60 s remain (G426). NECTAR must enter without a tool, by a same-color DRIVE TEAM member, and contact the tile in the LOADING ZONE before touching anything else (G427).

### Qualification Ranking Sort Order
1. Ranking Score (average RP)
2. Average alliance match points *minus* fouls
3. Average number of TIPS
4. Average AUTO points
5. Random

Playoffs are a double-elimination bracket; disqualifications in playoffs apply to the whole alliance.

## 5. Penalties

| Penalty | Effect |
|---|---|
| VERBAL WARNING | Warning from event staff / Head Referee |
| **MINOR FOUL** | **+5 points** to opponent |
| **MAJOR FOUL** | **+20 points** to opponent |
| YELLOW CARD | Warning; two yellows in a tournament phase = RED CARD |
| RED CARD | DISQUALIFIED for the match (0 match points, 0 RP in quals) |
| DISABLED | Robot must stop for the rest of the match |

Timing words: **MOMENTARY** < ~3 s, **CONTINUOUS** > ~10 s, **REPEATED** = more than once per match. **STRATEGIC** = done to gain a competitive advantage (deliberate or reckless); accidents exploited for advantage become STRATEGIC. Yellow cards clear at the end of Practice, Qualification, and division Playoff phases.

## 6. Key Game Rules (G)

### Pre-Match
- **G301** Don't delay matches. Verbal warning → Major Foul; disabled if not ready 2 min after that.
- **G303/G304 Robot start conditions:** on your alliance's side (red A–C, blue D–F), touching the perimeter wall, not in the LOADING ZONE, not touching a FLOWER, within STARTING CONFIGURATION, contacting **exactly 4 pre-loaded POLLEN**, motionless after INIT, correct-color ROBOT SIGNS.
- **G305** An OpMode must be selected and INIT'd. AUTO OpModes need the 30-second timer enabled. Teams without an auto should make a trivial AUTO OpMode that auto-loads TeleOp.
- **G302** Items at the field must fit in the alliance area, not exceed 6 ft 6 in tall, not communicate outside the arena, and not jam other teams' sensors (e.g., fake AprilTags, bright lights).

### AUTO
- **G401** No touching robot or console during AUTO except ▶ start at match start, ■ stop, or for safety.
- **G402** Don't disrupt the opponent's AUTO. Crossing into the opponent's half during AUTO is risky and may be deemed STRATEGIC. *Major Foul.*

### Transitions
- **G403** Robots motionless in the 8 s AUTO→TELEOP gap. If your TeleOp INIT twitches actuators, wait until TELEOP starts to press INIT.
- **G404** Robots motionless after 0:00 until referee signals.

### Scoring Elements
- **G405** Don't deliberately eject elements from the field. *Major Foul per element.*
- **G406** Don't damage or make a mess in the arena.
- **G407 Max 4 elements CONTROLLED at a time.** 5+ invites STRATEGIC scrutiny. Add guards so balls can't get stuck on the robot. Bulldozing, deflecting, and already-LAUNCHED balls do not count as CONTROL.
- **G408** Don't CONTROL opponent NECTAR.
- **G409** Don't catch/deflect elements falling from a tipping HIVE before they hit something else. Don't camp under the HIVE.
- **G410 NECTAR into FLOWERS only in the last 60 s.** *Major Foul per NECTAR.*
- **G411** No hoarding — don't STRATEGICALLY block opponent access to elements. *Major Foul + Yellow Card.*

### Robot
- **G412** Robot must be under control / not a hazard outside the field.
- **G413** Stop when instructed.
- **G414** Team number and alliance color must remain identifiable.
- **G415** No grabbing, attaching to, entangling with, or hanging from arena elements (a concave alignment shape around a FLOWER is fine).
- **G416** Stay within the expansion limit; don't detach parts.
- **G417 Don't meddle with the HIVE** — only LAUNCHING into the upward CELL may move it. No ramming the frame, no launching at the outside of a CELL, no impeding the opponent's tip.
- **G418 Don't meddle with FLOWERS** — only enter through the top, only remove POLLEN from the bottom. No shaking, pulling through the sides, forcing NECTAR out, or ramming the wall to descore.

### Opponent Interaction (G419/G420 are mutually exclusive; most punitive applies)
- **G419** Don't damage or functionally impair an opponent. Exposed switches, wiring, and delicate intakes are at your own risk — *build robust*.
- **G420** Don't tip or entangle an opponent. Incidental tipping from normal contact is not STRATEGIC.
- **G421 3-second PIN limit.** *Major Foul + another every 3 s.* Pin ends when robots separate 2 ft for 3 s, either moves 2 ft from the pin origin for 3 s, or the pinner gets pinned.

### Humans
- **G422** Stay in your ALLIANCE AREA. You may retrieve your own NECTAR that you can reach without leaving.
- **G423** DRIVE COACHES and other teams may not touch gamepads (they may hold the DS, select OpModes, and press INIT/▶/■).
- **G424** DRIVE COACHES may not touch scoring elements.
- **G425** Don't contact a robot, a ball touching a robot/tile, a field element, or disrupt scoring.
- **G426/G427** NECTAR entry rules (see §4). *Minor Foul per NECTAR.*
- **G428** Don't remove elements from the field. *Minor Foul per element.*

## 7. Robot Construction Rules (R)

### Size & Structure
- **R102 Starting configuration: 18 in × 18 in × 18 in cube.** Fully stationary and self-contained. Pre-loaded POLLEN may extend outside.
- **R103** Robot must be self-supporting in the sizing box; an OpMode may hold servos/motors in position during INIT.
- **R104 No weight limit.**
- **R105 Expansion limit: 18 in × 24 in × 29 in tall** (29 in is always vertical). Must be a *mechanical* limit, not software. Robot must remain one assembly — no deliberately detaching parts. Multiple independent mechanisms that could extend out both sides simultaneously are measured at their combined max.
- **R201–R204** No damaging/messy features, no vision-blocking shields, no AprilTag-mimicking imagery, no flashing lights > 5 Hz, no floor-grabbing or suction downforce, must be removable from field without power.

### Fabrication
- **R101** The robot and its MAJOR MECHANISMS must be built by the team.
- **R301** COTS mechanisms purpose-built for a game task are prohibited (except COTS drive chassis and official StarterBots).
- **R303** COTS mechanisms must be single degree of freedom (exceptions: ratchets, omni/mecanum wheels, dead-wheel odometry, U-joints, ball joints).
- **R304** Prior-season software, designs, and fabricated parts may be reused.
- **R305** Scoring elements / replicas may not be part of the robot.

### Robot Signs (R401–R403)
- At least **2 signs**, ≥ 90° apart, ≥ 6.5 in × 2.5 in, robust, frame-supported.
- Solid **red or blue** opaque rectangle (≥ 6.5 in × 2.5 in) for alliance color; reversible signs must not show the other color.
- **White Arabic team numbers ~2.25 in tall**, ≥ 0.25 in margin, not vertically stacked, not powered/illuminated.

### Motors & Servos
- **R501** Only listed 12 V DC motors: AndyMark NeveRest, goBILDA Yellow Jacket 520x / 5000 series, Modern Robotics/MATRIX, NFR Yuksel, REV HD Hex, REV Core Hex, Studica Maverick, SWYFT Spike, TETRIX MAX / TorqueNADO, WATTOS Stingray.
- **R502** Servos: ≤ 8 W mechanical output @ 6 V (0.25 × stall torque × no-load speed) and within stall-current limit; linear servos ≤ 1 A @ 6 V. Check the Inspection Quick Reference for pre-approved servos.
- **R503 Max 8 motors and 8 servos** across all configurations.
- **R504** Actuators may not be modified (except mounting, leads, manufacturer-specified servo mods, repairs).
- **R505** Actuators must be driven by approved devices: REV Control/Expansion Hub ports (2 motors or 2 servos per port), REV SPARKmini, REV Servo Power Module, REV Servo Hub, goBILDA Servo Power Injector, Studica Servo Power Block.
- **R506** No relays, electromagnets, or solenoids.

### Power
- **R601 Exactly one approved 12 V NiMH battery** (AndyMark am-5290, goBILDA 3100-0012-0020, Matrix 14-0014, REV-31-1302, Studica 70025, TETRIX W39057, WATTOS WT-NMH1230). Fuse may be replaced with a 20 A ATM mini blade; connectors may be swapped (Anderson, XT30, etc.).
- **R602** Other batteries only for isolated peripherals (USB packs ≤ 100 Wh, GoPro-style cameras).
- **R603** One approved main power switch (AndyMark, goBILDA Floodgate, REV, Studica, TETRIX, WATTOS) controlling all power; mount it protected from robot contact.
- **R604** Don't modify fuses. **R605** Frame is not a current path; only approved resistive grounding straps.
- **R606** Electronics and controller lights must be visible for inspection.
- **R607/R612** Custom circuits may not output regulated > 5 V (LEDs excepted) and may not alter critical power paths (no boost/buck on main paths; no goBILDA Servo Travel Tuner).
- **R609 Wire gauge:** 12 V main / motor power ≥ **18 AWG**; PWM/servo/LED/≤10 A circuits ≥ **22 AWG**; signal-level (I2C, DIO, analog, encoders) ≥ **28 AWG**.
- **R610 Wire colors** on 12 V and 5 V aux buses: positive = red/yellow/white/brown/black-with-stripe; negative = black/blue.
- **R613** Don't cross-wire power between hubs/ports; 6 V servo power only for servos.

### Control System
- **R701** One ROBOT CONTROLLER: **REV Control Hub** (or Android phone + Expansion Hub), plus at most **one additional Expansion Hub**.
- **R702** No modifying coprocessor software, except natively supported programmable vision coprocessors — currently only the **Limelight 3A**. (OpenMV, OAK-1, Limelight 3G are prohibited.)
- **R704 Wi-Fi:** only the RC↔DS link. Laptops disconnected during matches. **No streaming/logging services over Wi-Fi during matches (FTC Dashboard, FTControl Panels, etc. are prohibited); no continuous video stream.**
- **R705** Name devices `<team>-RC` and `<team>-DS` (spares: `<team>-A-RC`).
- **R707/R708 USB is for vision only:** UVC webcams (single image sensor, no stereo) and approved vision coprocessors, plus USB hubs and an Expansion Hub.
- **R709** Self-contained recording cameras OK with wireless off.
- **R710** Lasers only if part of a sensor, Class I/Exempt, non-visible.
- **R711** Change the Control Hub Wi-Fi password; Wi-Fi on, Bluetooth off; DS has no remembered networks besides the RC.

### Pneumatics
- **R801** No pneumatic actuators, compressors, vacuums, or high-speed blowers. Manufacturer-sealed gas shocks/springs are OK. Flywheels/rollers are fine.

### Operator Console
- **R901** One powered-on DRIVER STATION device (REV Driver Hub recommended). Spares allowed if powered off.
- **R902** DS touch screen must be accessible and visible.
- **R903** Console ≤ **3 ft × 1.5 ft × 2 ft**; > 20 lb invites scrutiny.
- **R904** No other wireless in the console (no wireless gamepads, no Bluetooth).

## 9. Strategy Notes for This Team

Derived from the rules above — see [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md) for our optimization priorities.

- **Tips are the big money:** 20 pts each in both AUTO and TELEOP, plus two RP thresholds (4 and 7 tips). Fast, reliable launch cycles into the upward CELL are the core of the game. Minimum cycle time directly maps to score.
- **AUTO:** LEAVE (3) + PARK (5) + tips (20 each). SWARM RP needs 16 combined LEAVE+PARK points across the alliance (e.g., both robots LEAVE + PARK in auto = 16 exactly). Stay on your side (A–C red / D–F blue).
- **Preload:** every robot starts touching exactly 4 POLLEN — design to launch those immediately.
- **Carry limit is 4 balls** — build guards so stray balls can't land on top of the robot (G407, G409).
- **Endgame at 1:00:** NECTAR into FLOWERS. Top-most NECTAR = ownership (2 pts per ball in that FLOWER); bottom-most NECTAR = 5 pt bonus. Timing the first NECTAR drop right after 1:00 matters; placing early is a 20 pt Major Foul per NECTAR.
- **PARK** in the LOADING ZONE at the end for 5 pts.
- **No FTC Dashboard or other Wi-Fi streaming during matches** (R704.D) — plan telemetry/tuning workflows accordingly.
- Build robust: BIOBUZZ is explicitly described as a high-contact game.
